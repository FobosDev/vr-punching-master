    How to play

    • Pink targets → punch with your left glove. Cyan targets → right glove. Gold → either.
    • Hit them as they cross the glowing strike zone in front of you. A real punch counts; a gentle touch does not.
    • Red spiked bombs: don't touch. Red walls: lean or duck out of the way.
    • Point at menus with a controller and pull the trigger. Press B or Y to pause. Hand tracking works too: pinch to select.
    • Needs a Meta Quest 3 (or any WebXR headset) over HTTPS. Passthrough mode uses the headset cameras so you can play in your room.
    Built with Three.js & WebXR. Everything runs locally in your browser. Source
